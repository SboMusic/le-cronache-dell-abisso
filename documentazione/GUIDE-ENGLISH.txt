CHRONICLES OF THE ABYSS - VERSION 1.0.0

START AND LANGUAGE
Run ChroniclesOfTheAbyss.exe in the portable/installed game, or the launcher in the project folder.
Click Italiano / English at the top right of the main menu or pause menu.
The language changes immediately and is remembered at the next launch.
The internal executable and existing save folder keep their old names for compatibility.

MODES
Start new campaign: choose Humans or Dark Kin. Each new game creates a separate campaign
with its own progress, manual save and autosave. The dark campaign uses the existing
eleven maps with adapted factions, casters and story introductions; it is not yet a
separate story with exclusive maps.
Continue campaign: choose a save by faction, mission and date. Previous-version saves
remain available. Options: language and fullscreen/windowed. Prologue: a guided free
game with faction choice. Quit: closes the game.

CONTROLS
Left-click: select. Drag: group selection. Shift: add to selection.
Right-click: move, attack, gather or repair according to the target.
Workers can also gather by selecting a tree after selecting a worker.
Hold the middle mouse button and drag to pan. Moving the pointer alone does not pan.
WASD/arrows: pan. Mouse wheel: zoom. Minimap: click or drag to move instantly.
O: worker from the selected command hall. T: soldier from selected barracks.
B: barracks placement for selected worker. H: select combat units.
C: cancel production/research. U: select a nearby shore to land a transport's troops.
F3 units, F4 buildings, F5 research, F6 spells: paused catalogs with mission limits.
F9: save campaign. F10: load manual save, or autosave if no manual save exists.

ECONOMY AND BUILDINGS
Command hall trains workers only and offers town upgrades.
Barracks I trains soldiers; Barracks II requires a lumber mill and unlocks archers.
Barracks III requires Veteran Doctrine; it unlocks veteran rangers.
Farms add six population slots, up to 90. Units can train without a farm while starting population slots remain available.
Mission objectives do not add an extra recruitment lock. Both sides follow population and resource rules.
Lumber mill: wood depot with +2 wood per delivery, ranged upgrades and Forestry.
Forge: Weapons and Armor research. Constructing it alone does not buy upgrades.
Stable: knights. Sanctuary: healers, guardians and Veteran Doctrine.
Workshop: ballistae, saboteurs and scout airships. Academy: casters and magical research.
Aviary: war rocs. Watchtower: detection; upgrade to guard or artillery tower.
Shipyard: coastal construction, ships. Foundry: heavy ships and fleet upgrades.
Tanker: builds a platform on an oil deposit, then gathers oil from that platform.
Refinery: more oil per delivery. Palisades block land routes.
Workers are busy at their construction sites until the building is complete.
Gold mines and individual trees are finite and disappear when exhausted.
Forestry allows replanting trees. Explored terrain remains clear; hidden enemies
must be rediscovered by your troops. See mission briefings for exact unlocks.

TRANSPORTS AND SPELLS
Move a transport near shore. Select up to six land units and right-click the ship.
Move the ship near an island, select Land troops or U, then click nearby free shore.
Right-clicking a nearby shore with the selected transport also lands passengers.
Casters require mana and may need Arcane Knowledge. Select a caster, choose its spell
icon, then click a target within range. Healers automatically heal nearby allies.

CAMPAIGN
1. Build 2 farms, deliver 120 gathered gold and 80 wood, reach the marked village.
2. Finish a barracks, field 3 soldiers, defeat 3 sentries, reach the northeastern marker.
3. Lumber mill, Barracks II, 2 archers; clear and hold the archive 10 s; escort Seren home.
4. Command Hall II, stable, 2 knights, guard tower; hold the pass 90 s; destroy the barracks.
   Protect both your command hall and the Abyssal refugee shelter.
5. Shipyard, tanker, oil platform, stockpile 60 oil; frigate and transport; land and hold
   the island temple with 3 land combat units for 20 s. Workers/healers/ships do not count.
6. Sanctuary, healer, Veteran Doctrine, Barracks III, 2 rangers; hold each archive 8 s,
   then defend the central archive for 30 s.
7. Workshop and 2 ballistae; destroy 3 military depots, then the enemy command hall.
8. Foundry, battleship and transport; hold the lighthouse with 3 combat units for 20 s,
   then destroy the marked enemy harbor. Frigates detect submarines.
9. Command Hall III, academy, Arcane Knowledge, 2 arcanists; hold each seal 10 s,
   then defend the central circle for 45 s.
10. Second command hall, aviary and 2 war rocs; reclaim 2 outposts (12 s each),
    then destroy both marked advanced workshops.
Final war: economy, farms, workshop, academy and at least 20 combat units.
   The enemy attacks only after your preparation and at least 7 minutes.
   Destroy EVERY enemy unit and building in all three camps. Preserve the Worldstone.

SAVES
Autosave at mission start and victory is separate from the manual save slot.
Continue saved game chooses the latest manual save/autosave. F10 prefers manual.
New campaigns: %LOCALAPPDATA%/Frontiere/campagne/<id>. Old saves stay at the root.
Screen and language settings remain at %LOCALAPPDATA%/Frontiere.
The latest valid save of the chosen campaign is loaded; a corrupt slot falls back
to the other slot of that campaign.
Updates and uninstallation preserve player data. Old saves still load.

SHARING
Share Chronicles-of-the-Abyss-1.0.0-Windows-Setup.exe or Chronicles-of-the-Abyss-1.0.0-Windows-Portable.zip.
The game runs offline on Windows 10/11 without Visual Studio or paid software.
The package includes libraries, original art, licenses and corresponding source code.
