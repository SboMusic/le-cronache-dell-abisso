# Rocce delle prime mappe

Generato con image_gen integrato; PNG con alpha preservata, copiato in progetto/assets/mappe-v1/rocce-v1.png. Quattro varianti in atlas 2x2, usate dal renderer delle creste rocciose. Il terreno di navigazione rimane separato dalla decorazione.

## Prompt completo

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: terrain decoration sprite atlas for original isometric 2D fantasy RTS Frontiere. One transparent PNG, 2 columns and 2 rows of equal cells, four isolated rocky outcrops. Each cell one different natural connected cluster of rugged grey granite and weathered brown mountain boulders, viewed from elevated three-quarter southeast RTS camera, detailed hand painted pre-rendered late 1990s fantasy strategy game style, sharp textured stone facets, neutral daylight, moss very sparse, dark crevices. Rock formations rise into irregular low crags and must tile visually by overlap to make long impassable ridges. No gold, no metal, no buildings, no caves or mine doorways, no crystals, no plants, no ground rectangle, no terrain backdrop, no labels or grid lines. Every rock cluster fully contained with 15 percent transparent padding per cell, base centered. Real transparent alpha around all rocks; no brown or black backdrop. Cohesive realistic painterly game assets, not flat polygon icons.
