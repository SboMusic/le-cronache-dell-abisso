# Grafica arsenale v1

Generazione integrata ImageGen, sfondo trasparente, senza API a pagamento o script esterni. I prompt seguenti sono quelli delle generazioni riuscite. Gli output sono copiati nel progetto; nessun collegamento remoto è necessario al gioco.

## roster-units-v1.png — usato

One transparent sprite atlas for an original medieval RTS. Square image, exactly 4x4 equal cells, no lines or text. Sixteen separated complete unit sprites facing southeast, each centered in its cell with clear margins. Hand-painted elevated three-quarter view, teal cloth, pale steel, oak and ochre accents, consistent sunlight. Row1: bow archer; green ranger; armored horseback knight; guardian rider with hammer. Row2: wheeled ballista; demolition engineer with barrel; robed fire mage; dark spirit mage. Row3: healer with staff; small scout airship; giant eagle warbird; oil tanker boat. Row4: troop transport ship; light destroyer sailship; heavy cannon battleship; bronze submarine. Original designs, no franchise likeness. No scenery or ground. Entire figures and vehicles must stay inside their own equal cells. Genuine transparent background.
